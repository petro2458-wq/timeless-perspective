Your north star

This is your North Star.

The goal of this calculator is simple: focus on executing a well-worked process to take X high-quality trades per week. You cannot control outcomes — you cannot control which trades win or lose. What you can control is showing up with discipline and taking the right number of quality setups every single week. At a 40% win rate, the math takes care of itself over time. Your north star is the number of trades per week that your reverse-engineered goal demands. Execute that process — nothing more, nothing less.
