Column definitions

One definition, both traders. If we disagree about how to log something, the definition gets settled and written here before any more rows go in.

Qualifying rules — not logged
15m POI
The zone is a 15m point of interest. Pro internal order flow is confirmed per row in the confluence list rather than assumed.
Break of structure
The POI must lead to a break in structure. A zone that doesn't is not a POI and doesn't get a row.
Mitigated by price
Price must have traded into the zone.
The confluences
POI level
POI
Flip Zone — a zone that caused a failed reaction and flipped. Supply Zone — sell-side origin. Demand Zone — buy-side origin.
POI entry price
The entry side of the zone — where price first engages it.
POI invalidation price
The far side of the zone.
POI 1R
POI entry to POI invalidation. The zone's own height — the yardstick that puts ES, Gold and FX on one scale.
Failure (POI level)
Invalidation price touched, wick or close. This level measures the zone itself.
Meaningful reaction
POI MFE of 2R or better.
Swing Bias
Direction of the H4 swing structure, same read as Daily Trend. Long or Short.
MTF Bias
Direction of the 15m swing structure, same read as Daily Trend. Long or Short. The R sign now comes from the trade direction derived from Market Phase and the biases. See Market Phase definitions.
Market Phase & Daily reads
The trader picks the market phase that matches price right now, and the probability (High, Medium or Low) follows from that pick. These reads frame every phase: Daily Trend (Daily structure), Swing (H4 structure) and MTF (15m structure), plus Today's Bias, the trader's own read of the day.
Daily Trend
Daily chart input. Daily Trend is the Daily structure: Bullish, Bearish or Consolidating. It is a structural read of the larger trend, and the only place it changes probability is P1 · Well-Priced.
Today's Bias
Daily chart input. Today's Bias is the trader's discretionary read of the day: Bullish, Bearish or Unclear. It is the bias for the day, and it influences which trades the trader selects. It does not change the probability, the phase or the trade direction. It is recorded as a stat confluence so it can be studied later. For P3 it is a hint toward the Daily-supports call: matching the MTF favors the pullback, opposing the MTF leans against it.
Probability
Daily Trend changes the probability of one phase only: P1 · Well-Priced is High when Daily Trend matches Swing and MTF, and Medium when Daily Trend conflicts or is Consolidating. For P3, whether the Daily supports the pullback is a discretionary call, so P3 is split into two phases.
Entry level
Entry timeframe
The timeframe the entry trigger was read on — 1m, 3m, 5m or 15m.
Entry price
Where the entry filled. Entry R is measured from here, not from the POI edge.
Stop
One stop per trade. Flip Zone: below the flip zone. Flip Zone Sweep: below the sweep low. EM3 Aggressive: trader discretion. The liquidation candle is the stop, especially when the zone is too wide, but the POI invalidation is used instead when it is risk-reward friendly and close enough.
B/E trigger price
The price at which the stop was moved to breakeven.
MFE / MAE
Best and worst excursion after entry, logged as prices. The grid converts them to R against the stop.
Outcome
Win, Loss or Breakeven.
B/E Trades (W/L)
Breakeven rows only. Win: it went on to reach target. Loss: it would have stopped out anyway. This is how the data judges whether moving to breakeven pays.
Entry models
Flip Zone
Entry trigger is an MSS confirmed, then price retraces back to the flip zone. No sweep is needed.
Flip Zone Sweep
Same as Flip Zone but a sweep is required. The sweep can occur before or after the MSS.
EM1/2 Aggressive
Enter once push liquidity is taken into a high-probability POI — a supply/demand zone or flip zone that broke structure — where most or all visible liquidity targets have been swept. No fMS required; the push liquidity clearance is the trigger.
EM3 Aggressive
Entry on the fMS-low sweep, taken immediately.
EM3 Conservative
A second entry on the same POI, taken after an EM3 Aggressive. The EM3 Aggressive may still be open, or may have already taken profits. It comes in two types: Flip Zone and Flip Zone Sweep, defined above.
In Zone Push Liq.
Yes if liquidity or inducement was pushed (taken) inside the zone before the move. No if it was not.
Entry-level failure
A 3m close beyond the POI — different from the POI level, where a touch is enough.
Attempt cap
Maximum 2 attempts or 2 losses per POI.
