Column definitions

One definition, both traders. If we disagree about how to log something, the definition gets settled and written here before any more rows go in.

Qualifying rules — not logged
15m POI
The zone is a 15m point of interest. Pro internal order flow is confirmed per row in the confluence list rather than assumed.
Break of structure
The POI must lead to a break in structure. A zone that doesn't is not a POI and doesn't get a row.
Mitigated by price
Price must have traded into the zone.
The seven confluences + inducement
POI level
POI
Flip Zone — a zone that caused a failed reaction and flipped. Supply Zone — sell-side origin. Demand Zone — buy-side origin.
POI entry price
The entry side of the zone — where price first engages it.
POI invalidation price
The far side of the zone.
POI 1R
POI entry to POI invalidation. The zone's own height — the yardstick that puts ES, Gold and FX on one scale.
Failure (POI level)
Invalidation price touched, wick or close. This level measures the zone itself.
Meaningful reaction
POI MFE of 2R or better.
H4 bias
H4 directional bias at the time — long or short.
H1 bias
H1 directional bias at the time — long or short. Logged alongside H4 so alignment across timeframes becomes a filter rather than an assumption.
15m bias
Long or short — the direction the POI is traded in, and the sign used for every R calculation on the row.
Entry level
Entry timeframe
The timeframe the entry trigger was read on — 1m, 3m, 5m or 15m.
Entry price
Where the entry filled. Entry R is measured from here, not from the POI edge.

Technical target
The structural profit target for that entry, logged as a price.
B/E trigger price
The price at which the stop was moved to breakeven.
MFE / MAE
Best and worst excursion after entry, logged as prices. The grid converts them to R against both stops.
Outcome
Win, Loss or Breakeven.
After B/E
Only for breakeven rows: Worked means it went on to reach target, Failed means it would have stopped out anyway. This is how the data judges whether moving to breakeven pays.
Entry models
EM1/2 Aggressive
Enter once push liquidity is taken into a high-probability POI — a supply/demand zone or flip zone that broke structure — where most or all visible liquidity targets have been swept. No fMS required; the push liquidity clearance is the trigger.
EM3 Aggressive
Entry on the fMS-low sweep, taken immediately.
Fake shift
For EM3 entries: which structure the fake market shift broke — fractal or strong.
EM3 Conservative
The flip zone sweep following a working EM3 Aggressive — Entry 2, with Entry 1's stop moving to breakeven. The Flip zone column records whether that zone was already swept or still needed to be swept.
FZ Sweep
Flip zone swept with a clear sweep on 1m, taken alone. Terminal — no EM3 Aggressive or Conservative follows it.
Push liquidity
Whether pre-BOS liquidity existed to fuel the move into or through the POI. Drives the top branch of the decision tree.
Entry-level failure
A 3m close beyond the POI — different from the POI level, where a touch is enough.
Attempt cap
Maximum 2 attempts or 2 losses per POI.
